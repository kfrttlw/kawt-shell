kawt VGA (KawtVGA-Regular.ttf, KawtVGA-Bold.ttf)

The pixels: the IBM VGA 8x16 text mode font, with latin, greek and cyrillic added, as BDF by
farsil: https://github.com/farsil/ibmfonts (bdf/iv8x16u.bdf), "BDF conversion (c) 2016 farsil,
license: CC BY-SA 4.0".

Changed by kawt-shell (tools/vgafont.py): made a TrueType font, each pixel a square on a grid of
one screen pixel at 16 px, so that kitty can use it; a bold drawn from it (each pixel twice, the
second one to the right).
